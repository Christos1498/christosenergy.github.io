Christfield Dynamics — Technical Appendix A
A comprehensive mathematical research program extending Christfield Dynamics through 2,500 numbered sections of formal analysis, worked models, and proposed experimental tests. Develops stochastic path measures and Doob transforms, information geometry and Fisher metrics, phase synchronization and toroidal diffusion, constrained control and thermodynamics, multiscale network stability, geometric phase, gauge structures, and topological dynamics. Establishes explicit null models, identifiability conditions, measurement protocols, and falsification criteria to distinguish conventional physical mechanisms from proposed Christfield-specific effects. The mathematical constructions are presented as formal results under stated assumptions; the proposed awareness-to-coherence coupling and any new physical mechanism remain hypotheses requiring independent experimental confirmation.
