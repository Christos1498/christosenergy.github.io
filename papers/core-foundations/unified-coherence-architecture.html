Abstract

Modern physics operates across multiple highly successful but fundamentally fragmented frameworks. General Relativity models gravity as spacetime curvature. Quantum Mechanics describes matter through probabilistic wavefunctions. Thermodynamics establishes entropy as the arrow of time. Each succeeds within its domain. Each fails at the intersection with the others.

This paper presents the Unified Coherence Architecture (UCA), a multi-scale framework in which time is reinterpreted as a primary dynamical field rather than a coordinate dimension, spatial separation emerges from phase differences in the temporal field, and system organization is governed by a coherence scalar field representing phase alignment density. The framework recovers established results from General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics in appropriate limits while providing a consistent interpretation of phenomena including time dilation, quantum entanglement, dark matter anomalies, cosmological large-scale structure, and civilizational phase transitions.

The UCA is organized into three causal layers: (1) Toroidal Geometry governing global spacetime structure; (2) Coherence Field Dynamics governing local metric modifications and phase evolution; and (3) Cross-Scale Emergence governing biological, social, and civilizational behavior as natural consequences of the same underlying principles. A complete kinematic language of eight motion operators acting on phase is derived. Explicit falsification criteria and discriminating predictions are provided.

The Prime Axiom of the CTF — v⃗ = −∇C, meaning coherence moves the craft — governs all scales from quantum to cosmic.
