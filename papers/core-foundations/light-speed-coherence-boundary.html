Abstract

The speed of light — 299,792,458 meters per second in vacuum — is treated in standard physics as a fundamental constant of nature whose value is given but not explained. Maxwell's equations predict it from electromagnetic constants. Special Relativity makes it the universal speed limit. Quantum electrodynamics describes it as the propagation speed of photons. None of these frameworks explain what the speed of light actually is at its source.

This paper establishes the complete Light Speed as Coherence Boundary framework. The toroidal geometry of space is established as the foundational architecture — demonstrating that the standard Euclidean assumption of infinite flat space is a local approximation that breaks down at astronomical scales, producing the dark matter problem and other observational anomalies. The apparent faster-than-light phenomena of quantum entanglement and nonlocality are explained as operations at the coherence potential layer where spatial separation has already been reduced to zero through phase-matching.

The 4D Mirror Realm is described as the only dimensional layer without its own sun — because it is the reflection layer whose light comes from the three-dimensional reality it mirrors. The Sun is shown to be inside our torus — its apparent distance is a temporal field phase difference, not a physical gap.
