Abstract

This master engineering companion document presents complete manufacturing specifications for the seven Christos™ Cancer Device families. The portfolio spans the full range of coherence medicine delivery modalities: wearable resonator patches, structured healing fluids, frequency wands, portable coherence chambers, and distributed field arrays. Each device family specification includes the complete bill of materials, PCB layout logic, firmware architecture, quality control protocols, and regulatory pathway map. The seven device families are designed to address the five-pillar coherence medicine cancer protocol across 50+ cancer types as described in the Terminal Cancer Reversal Protocol Library. This document is the companion engineering reference to Papers 1–6 of the Christos™ Coherence Medicine Oncology Series, providing the manufacturing-ready technical layer that operationalizes the clinical frameworks described in those papers. The engineering specifications contained herein are provided to establish public prior art, support licensing discussions, and enable qualified contract manufacturers to evaluate production feasibility.
