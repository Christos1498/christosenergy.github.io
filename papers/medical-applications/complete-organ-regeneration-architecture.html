Abstract

This document is the complete internal manufacturing and clinical reference for the Christos™ Organ Regeneration System described in the companion paper MA-01: an exhaustive catalog covering all major organs of the human body across eleven biological systems, a corresponding structured-water healing fluid and gel formulary, a full set of organ-specific morphogenic resonator device specifications, a directory of coherence chamber protocols including an eleven-phase, full-body session sequence, and consolidated master reference tables.

Unlike MA-01, this document is a pure specification catalog rather than a narrative scientific paper: it contains no discussion of evidence, no citations, and no research proposals of its own. Its entire content, every frequency assignment, every fluid and gel formulation, every resonator specification, and every protocol timing, is held under NDA and is not disclosed in this public version.

I. What This Document Is

Where MA-01 makes the scientific case for the Organ Regeneration System, drawing on a substantial peer-reviewed evidence base across acoustic, photonic, electromagnetic, and structured-water regenerative medicine, this document is its complete internal build-out: the specific catalog a manufacturer or clinician would need to actually produce every fluid, gel, and device, and run every protocol, described at full implementation detail. It is organized as a pure reference catalog rather than an argued paper, and this page presents it accordingly, describing its scope and structure without reproducing its contents.

II. The Organ Master List

The catalog opens with a master list of the body's major organs, organized across eleven biological systems: circulatory and lymphatic, digestive, nervous, endocrine, respiratory, urinary, male reproductive, female reproductive, integumentary, skeletal and muscular, and sensory. For each organ, the source material assigns a proposed primary regenerative frequency and a qualitative regenerative-capacity assessment (high, moderate, or limited), forming the organizing reference that every fluid, gel, resonator, and chamber protocol in the rest of the document is built around. The complete organ list with its specific frequency assignments is not reproduced in this public version.

III. Healing Fluids and Gels

The catalog specifies a complete formulary of organ-specific healing fluids and companion gels, each named for its target organ or system, built on the same structured, deuterium-depleted water base and Solfeggio frequency-imprinting approach described conceptually in MA-01. Each formulation combines specific botanical extracts and nutritional compounds with a specific frequency-imprinting sequence and a specific dosing and application protocol. Every formulation's complete ingredient list, exact concentrations, frequency-imprinting sequence, and dosing protocol is a trade secret and is not disclosed in this public version. This information is not appropriate for self-directed compounding or dosing under any circumstance; formulation and dosing decisions require professional manufacturing oversight and individualized physician guidance.

IV. Morphogenic Resonators

The catalog specifies a complete set of organ-specific resonator devices, each defined by a proposed crystal type, a proposed center frequency, a specific body placement location, and a specific recommended wear duration, extending the general Morphogenic Resonator concept described in MA-01 to essentially every major organ and organ group in the body. The complete resonator specification table, including every crystal type, frequency, placement, and duration, is a trade secret and is not disclosed in this public version.

V. Chamber Protocol Directory

The catalog specifies a directory of coherence chamber protocols organized by organ system, plus a flagship eleven-phase, full-body regeneration protocol intended to sequence all nine Solfeggio frequencies and the Schumann resonance in a single extended session addressing every organ system in sequence. Each phase specifies a particular frequency, target organs, and coordinated fluid or environmental delivery, and the full protocol specifies detailed chamber environmental parameters (water bath conditions, mineral content, acoustic and photonic array specifications, and electromagnetic field generation equipment). The complete phase-by-phase protocol, every session timing, frequency, and environmental specification, is a trade secret and is not disclosed in this public version.

VI. Reference Tables

The catalog closes with consolidated master reference tables: a frequency map cross-referencing every organ system, a regeneration-capacity reference summarizing the qualitative assessments from Part II, and a proposed regeneration timeline reference. These are internal cross-reference tools built entirely from the specific, protected content described above, and are not reproduced in this public version.
