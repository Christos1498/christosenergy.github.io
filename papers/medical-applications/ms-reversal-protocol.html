The Complete Multiple Sclerosis Reversal Protocol

Demyelination Repair · Immune Regulation · Nervous System Regeneration · A Coherence-Based Approach

AuthorJoshua Farrior
OrganizationChristos™ Energy, Technology & Harmonic Design Consulting, LLC
PublishedApril 2026
SeriesTechnical White Paper Series — Volume II
Abstract

Multiple sclerosis (MS) affects approximately 2.9 million people globally and is conventionally characterized as an incurable progressive autoimmune disease for which management — not reversal — is the clinical standard. Patients are offered immunosuppressive drugs that reduce relapse frequency but do not repair demyelination, restore neurological function, or address the underlying biological failure points that drive disease progression.

This paper presents the Christos™ Complete MS Reversal Protocol — a five-pillar, 24-month coherence-based framework targeting five biological failure points simultaneously: blood-brain barrier (BBB) permeability, myelin sheath degradation, oligodendrocyte precursor cell (OPC) suppression, immune dysregulation, and mitochondrial failure. The core scientific premise is that OPCs — the biological machinery for myelin regeneration — are present in MS lesions but suppressed from completing remyelination by the coherence environment of the lesion. Restoring that coherence environment activates endogenous remyelination.

Five specialized frequency-imprinted healing fluids are introduced: BBB-Flux (blood-brain barrier restoration), Myelin-Flux (oligodendrocyte activation and myelin regeneration), ImmunoFlux (immune coherence and regulation), MitoFlux (mitochondrial energy restoration), and NeuroFlux (axonal signal coherence). These are delivered alongside PEMF therapy at OPC-activation frequencies, photobiomodulation targeting the central nervous system, high-dose vitamin D3 and alpha-lipoic acid protocols, lion's mane mushroom neurotrophin support, and targeted mineral restoration.

The scientific foundation draws on peer-reviewed research in oligodendrocyte precursor cell biology, remyelination mechanisms, vitamin D and immune regulation in MS, alpha-lipoic acid neuroprotection, lion's mane nerve growth factor research, and the established biophysics of PEMF and photobiomodulation. The protocol proposes a 200-participant, 24-month randomized controlled trial with MRI lesion load, EDSS score, and HRV coherence as primary endpoints.
