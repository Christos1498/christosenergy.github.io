Abstract

Biological Interface Systems presents the family of coherence-based devices bridging human biological field states to the broader Christos platform — enabling real-time monitoring, optimization, and therapeutic delivery through a unified personal coherence interface architecture. These devices form the sensory and delivery layer of the Christos medical system at the individual scale.

The Harmonic Morphoscope is the primary diagnostic instrument of the biological interface family — a multi-modality biofield assessment instrument integrating SQUID magnetometry for cardiac and neural magnetic field mapping, terahertz field sensing for tissue hydration and molecular structure assessment, quantum-limited biophoton detection for cellular coherence state measurement, and coherence gradient mapping for whole-body field topology visualization. Together these four sensing modalities provide the complete dimensional health assessment that the Christos medical system requires for individualized protocol design.

The C1 Coherence Scanner is the personal-scale portable version of the Harmonic Morphoscope — designed for home use and continuous monitoring. It provides real-time CI scoring, organ-system coherence tracking, and protocol guidance based on the individual's current coherence state. The C0 Coherence Diagnostician is the clinical-grade desk instrument providing full Harmonic Morphoscope capability in a form factor suited for clinical practice.

The Quantum Biofield Interface (QBI) represents the advanced end of the biological interface family — a direct neural-biofield interface enabling bidirectional communication between the human nervous system and the CQI network. The QBI translates the user's biofield coherence state into CQI network signals for authentication, communication, and navigation, and delivers CQI network responses back to the user through precise biofield modulation.
