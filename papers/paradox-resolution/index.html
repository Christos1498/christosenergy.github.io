The Paradox Resolution Papers apply the Christos Theoretical Framework — five resolution moves built on the toroidal coherence architecture — to the deepest unsolved problems in physics, cosmology, biology, psychology, linguistics, social science, philosophy, ecology, economics, aesthetics, and meta-theory.
