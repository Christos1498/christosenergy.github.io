The Paradox Resolution Papers

When the Question Completes

These are not the same as the main Christos™ white papers. Those papers build devices, establish protocols, and document inventions. These papers do something different — they point toward where the answer lives.

Most of the great paradoxes of human thought — from the arrow of time to free will to the hard problem of consciousness to the Fermi paradox — are not unsolvable mysteries. They are questions that appear unanswerable because they are being asked inside a framework too small to hold the answer. What looks like a contradiction from inside the box resolves when you can see the whole shape of the box.

Each paper takes one of humanity's deepest unresolved paradoxes and shows the pathway. Not the complete solution — I'll leave that to the world. But the direction, the framework move, the coherence architecture that makes resolution possible. I didn't solve these problems. I completed the questions.

117 papers published across five volumes. Every claim falsifiable. Every limitation documented. Seven papers carry the [corrected] designation — overclaimed mechanisms tightened per internal peer review.

The Master Framework

Every paradox in this series resolves by one or more of five structural moves.

1
Complete the Torus
Find the Saturnalia return current. Every outward Christos expression has a return flow. The paradox disappears when the missing half of the cycle is identified.
2
Name the Threshold
Identify C_critical. Many paradoxes are threshold phenomena — they appear as contradictions below the threshold and resolve cleanly above it.
3
Phi-Ratio Asymmetry
When symmetry appears broken, look for the phi-ratio relationship. Apparent asymmetry is often the expression of phi-ratio geometry in the underlying field.
4
Find the Boundary
Identify which dimension the paradox is occurring at. Many paradoxes sit at the interface between dimensional layers and appear contradictory only there.
5
Attractor Dynamics
Replace static analysis with dynamic attractor modeling. Many paradoxes dissolve when the question shifts from "what is it?" to "what basin is it in?"

On the [corrected] designation: Seven papers in this series carry a peer-review correction label. These are papers where specific mechanistic claims were tightened: unvalidated mechanisms removed, speculative extensions clearly labeled Tier C, and falsifiable predictions preserved intact. The Christos™ framework and all five resolution moves remain unchanged. The corrections reflect intellectual honesty about what has been established versus what remains hypothetical.
