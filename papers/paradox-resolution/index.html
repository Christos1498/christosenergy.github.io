The Paradox Resolution Papers apply the Christos Theoretical Framework — five resolution moves built on the toroidal coherence architecture — to the deepest unsolved problems across physics, cosmology, mathematics, biology, psychology, linguistics, social science, philosophy, ecology, economics, aesthetics, and meta-theory. Five moves resolve all of them. All 118 papers are full open access. All are the original work of Joshua Farriar, Christos™ Energy, Technology & Harmonic Design Consulting, LLC.
