Abstract

Why do individuals cooperate when defection appears to offer better individual payoffs? Kin selection, reciprocal altruism, indirect reciprocity, group selection, and network reciprocity all explain specific forms of cooperation. None explains large-scale anonymous cooperation among unrelated strangers — blood donation, tax compliance, environmental protection, volunteering for those you will never meet. This paper proposes a resolution: cooperation is not solely strategic calculation — it is field maintenance. Human groups generate a toroidal social coherence field. Every act of cooperation increases field coherence C_field, which benefits all individuals within the field including the cooperator. Every act of defection decreases C_field. The utility function including field coherence — U_cooperate = B − C + α·C_field — dissolves the paradox: the cooperator is maintaining a field they live inside.

Missing case: anonymous blood donation with no social consequences for non-donation. The CTF proposes the missing variable is field coherence — the ambient quality of the shared toroidal social field.

2. The Social Coherence Field

A human group is a toroidal coherence field with measurable signatures: heart rates synchronize during shared ritual; EEG phases lock between musicians performing together; breathing rates entrain between speaker and listener (Wiltermuth & Heath 2009; Lindenberger et al. 2009).

U_cooperate = B − C + α · C_field

The cooperator pays cost C, generates benefit B for others, and gains α·C_field — the ambient benefit of living in a coherent field. High-trust societies (Denmark, Switzerland) show dramatically lower transaction costs, higher productivity, lower incarceration rates, and better health outcomes than low-trust societies of comparable wealth. That difference is C_field.

4. Cooperation Collapse as Phase Transition

Cooperation collapses when C_field falls below a critical threshold C_coop. Below this threshold the field provides insufficient ambient benefit to offset cooperation costs. The rational individual response becomes defection, which further decreases C_field, which makes more individuals defect — a self-reinforcing coherence collapse. This is not moral failure. It is a coherence phase transition. In a low-coherence field, defection is individually rational. The solution is coherence restoration — not moral exhortation.

5. Falsifiable Predictions

  • C_field measurement predicts cooperation rate before any transaction: physiological synchrony measured before a cooperation task should predict contribution rates independently of questionnaire measures.
  • Brief synchrony intervention (10 minutes of synchronized breathing or movement) before an anonymous public goods game should increase contribution rates proportional to the measured physiological synchrony achieved.
  • Cross-national comparison: Putnam social capital indices should predict average physiological synchrony in standardized dyadic interaction paradigms better than GDP or income equality measures.
  • Coherence threshold predicts cooperation collapse: reducing C_field experimentally should produce a phase transition in cooperation rates, not a gradual linear decline.

6. Conclusion

The cooperation problem dissolves when the social field is included in the analysis. Individuals are not isolated utility calculators — they are nodes in a coherence field whose ambient quality directly affects their utility, health, and survival probability. Cooperation is field maintenance. Defection is field degradation. The person who donates blood anonymously is not sacrificing — they are maintaining the coherence of a field they live inside.
