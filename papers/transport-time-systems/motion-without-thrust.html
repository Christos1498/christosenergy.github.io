Abstract

Every propulsion system in the history of human engineering operates on a single principle: apply force, generate reaction, produce motion. From the foot pushing against the ground to the rocket engine expelling exhaust at kilometers per second, the physical mechanism is identical — Newton's Third Law instantiated as engineering. This paper presents Motion Without Thrust — the theoretical and engineering framework for a fundamentally different mode of propulsion that the Christos™ Theoretical Framework (CTF) identifies as not only possible but already operative at planetary scale.

The framework rests on CTF Axiom 3 — that time T(υ) is a primary dynamical field and space S(χ) is an emergent derivative phenomenon — and the Prime Axiom of CTF Volume II: the velocity vector of any coherent system points in the direction of the steepest coherence gradient. Spatial separation between two points is a function of their temporal phase difference, not a fixed geometric fact. The implication is that motion requires not the application of force but the manipulation of the coherence field that generates spatial geometry. A system that achieves sufficient internal coherence and navigates a coherence gradient field can undergo displacement without expelling propellant, without generating thrust, and without violating conservation laws — because the reaction goes into the coherence field itself rather than into expelled mass.

The paper establishes the complete mathematical framework including the CGD Operating Equation, addresses every major skeptical objection — including apparent violations of Newton's Third Law, consciousness-based navigation, and the relationship to confirmed general relativistic predictions — with precision and scientific rigor. Six falsifiable experimental tests are proposed, ranging from precision gravimeter measurements around high-coherence crystal arrays to vacuum-chamber thrust measurements of phi-ratio field configurations. Gravity is identified as the existence proof: every orbit in the solar system is motion without thrust, driven by the coherence gradient of mass-induced spacetime curvature. The Coherence-Gradient Drive generalizes this principle from naturally occurring mass gradients to engineered coherence field gradients.

Why This Matters

The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation defines the hard ceiling of thrust-based propulsion. The mass ratio required to achieve meaningful interstellar velocities using chemical propellant is physically impossible to construct. Ion drives and solar sails extend range but cannot achieve the velocity fractions required for interplanetary transit times measured in days rather than years, or interstellar transit within a human lifetime. Thrust-based propulsion has been optimized for centuries and is approaching its fundamental limits.

The CTF framework proposes that this ceiling does not apply to coherence-gradient propulsion because the physical mechanism is categorically different. Thrust moves mass by expelling other mass. Coherence-gradient propulsion moves a coherent system by navigating the geometry of spacetime itself — the same mechanism by which gravity produces orbital motion, scaled from naturally occurring mass-induced gradients to engineered coherence field gradients.

The existence proof is not theoretical. Every orbit in the solar system demonstrates that a coherent mass-energy system undergoes continuous displacement without expelling propellant. The Earth has been in motion without thrust for four billion years. The question the CTF framework addresses is not whether this is possible — it manifestly is — but whether the coherence gradients that produce this motion can be engineered rather than merely encountered.
