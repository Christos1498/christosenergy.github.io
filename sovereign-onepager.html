The Problem

Most organizations have accepted a significant portion of their operational cost as unavoidable. It isn't.

Every business runs on five organizational systems: flow, boundary regulation, feedback, memory, and regeneration. When any one breaks down, value stops circulating and starts leaking out of the system.

The leakage is typically invisible on the income statement because it has been normalized into the cost structure. It shows up instead as unexplained overhead growth, persistent margin pressure, supply chain friction, and workforce churn that won't stop.
